# Moodify 🎧
**Your Music Knows You — Now You Can Know Yourself.**

Moodify is a personal emotional insight tool that analyzes your Spotify listening habits to reveal your mood patterns, energy cycles, and emotional trends. It turns your music data into a **psychology-based profile** that helps you understand how you feel — even before you say it.

---

##  Why Moodify Matters
We listen to music every day — when happy, stressed, focused, or nostalgic.  
But we rarely stop to ask:

> *What does my music say about me?*

Spotify already collects this listening data, but users never see the emotional meaning behind it. Moodify reveals the hidden emotional fingerprint inside your playlists.

---

##  Who It's For
Moodify is designed for:
- People who love self-reflection and emotional growth
- Music lovers who want deeper meaning from listening
- Anyone tracking mental well-being or personal habits
- Creatives, students, professionals — **everyone who listens to music**

##  Use Cases
"When am I most productive? Should I schedule deep work for mornings or afternoons?"
"Why do I feel drained on Thursdays? Is there a pattern I'm missing?"
"Am I actually stressed, or does it just feel that way?"
"What does my music say about my personality?"

---

##  Key Features
| Feature | What It Does |
|--------|---------------|
| **Mood Dashboard** | Shows your current emotional state based on listening patterns |
| **Weekly Mood Summary** | Tracks your emotional highs and lows over time |
| **Emotional Timeline Graph** | Visualizes how your mood shifts over days/weeks |
| **Song-Based Mood Drivers** | Identifies songs affecting your energy and feelings |
| **Personalized Music Recommendations** | Suggests playlists based on emotional needs |

---

##  Technologies Used (Planned)
- **Frontend:** React / Next.js
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express
- **Data Processing:** Python (sentiment + tempo analysis)
- **Spotify Access:** Spotify Web API (OAuth login)
- **Database:** MongoDB or PostgreSQL


---

##  Privacy First
Your data stays yours:

- No listening history stored on our servers
- Analysis happens on-demand
- We never share your music taste with anyone
- Delete your account = delete all data instantly

We only store:

Your Spotify user ID (for authentication)
Generated insights (optional, encrypted)
Aggregated, anonymized patterns (for product improvement)
